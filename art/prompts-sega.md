# Prompty pro obrázky nových her (Higgsfield Nano Banana Pro, Unlimited mode)

Výstup: plné PNG do `art/raw/<soubor>` (mimo git). Archy 1:1 (2K), bannery 16:9 (2K).

Společný konec promptu archů (STYL_ARCH):

> Cute cartoon sprite sheet for a children's video game. Exactly 9 separate objects arranged in an invisible 3x3 grid,
> each object centered in its own cell with plenty of empty space around it, all objects similar size.
> Style: thick dark brown outline, flat bright cheerful colors, soft simple shading, friendly smiling faces.
> Plain pure white background, no text, no letters, no numbers, no grid lines, no frames, no ground shadows.

Společný konec promptu bannerů (STYL_BANNER):

> Bright cheerful cartoon illustration in the style of a children's game cover, 16:9. A happy little boy with short
> brown hair in a green t-shirt and a happy little girl with blonde pigtails in a pink dress. Big bold rounded
> white title letters with thick blue outline at the top, the title must be spelled exactly: "<NÁZEV>" (keep the Czech
> accents). Thin blue border around the picture. No other text.
